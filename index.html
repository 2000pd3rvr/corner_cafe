A neighbourhood cafe that feels like home.

Corner cafe serves Scottish and British classics from morning porridge to afternoon tea—haggis rolls, Cullen skink, steak & ale pie, warm scones and a proper brew. The counter faces the room so you can watch plates leave the kitchen, with soft seats for linger-over lunches and tea for two.

See more

You’ll find us at 9 Eskdail Court in Dalkeith, Midlothian—steps from the High Street. Walk-ins are welcome; mornings fill quickly for the full Scottish, and tea service is popular from midday onwards.

Scottish plates, British comfort

Drawn from Edinburgh’s British and Scottish cafe tables—farmhouse breakfasts, fish & chips, soup and bridies, bothy-style whisky notes, and afternoon cream tea with warm scones. We keep it seasonal, local where we can, and generous: breakfast through lunch into tea, every day we’re open.

See more

The cafe counter anchors the room so you can watch the kitchen turn out rolls, soups and trays of scones. Settle in for a quiet tea, a working lunch, or a catch-up over cranachan—walk-ins welcome at 9 Eskdail Court.
